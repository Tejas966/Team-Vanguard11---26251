        Calculations, decision rules and references

        Calculations and rules

        01 What-If Simulator

        Stock at arrival = inventory − consumption × (delivery time + delay), with consumption scaled by the change %. Time to stock-out = inventory ÷ consumption. Severity uses margin = time to stock-out − arrival day: ≥ 2 d Low; 0–2 d Watch; 0 to −2 d High; below −2 d Critical. Extra cost = shortage units airlifted at an illustrative ₹200 per kg.

        Stock-out probability

        Daily demand varies, by coefficient of variation (CV) per class: rations 0.08, fuel 0.15, medical 0.5, spares 0.6. Higher CV for intermittent items follows the spare-parts literature. Probability = 1 − Φ((stock − c·T) ÷ (CV·c·√T)), a normal approximation. The full build replaces this with Monte Carlo and conformal intervals.

        02 Ripple-Effect Analyzer

        The delay or shortage hits the source node's incoming delivery. Each depot then runs a dispatch queue: in date order it ships each child's scheduled load if stock allows. If not, that load waits for the depot's (delayed) resupply. A child's delay is passed on to its own children the same way, following real edges only. A post is red if stock runs out before its delivery, or the delivery covers under 2 days. It is amber if delayed, the margin is under 2 days, or post-delivery cover is under 5 days.

        03 Stress Test and decision rule

        1. Hard rule: a plan with fewer posts short wins.
        2. Otherwise lowest weighted score = wrisk·risk + wtime·time + wcost·cost, each scaled 0–1 against the worst plan. Risk = shortage post-days + 5 per post short.
        3. If no plan removes all shortages, the panel says so and computes what would close the gap.

        Plan B rules: reroute sends Depot-B resupply via Route-2, arriving day 5 at 60% of normal load. Airlift sends up to 2 sorties of 2,000 kg on day 1 to the most urgent red posts, ranked by priority × shortage. Reallocation moves surplus over 3 days' safety from a green neighbour along a permitted lateral link, arriving day 1.
